A few things

Which areas do you serve?

Swift Air Care serves homes in the Greater Toronto Area and nearby communities. Include your city and postal code in your request so we can confirm availability for your location.

Can I request a repair or a new installation?

Yes. You can ask about a heating or cooling problem, a replacement system, a new installation, or seasonal maintenance. Choose the closest service in the request form and tell us what you need.

Is my appointment confirmed when I send a request?

No. A service request is the first step. Your appointment is confirmed only after we contact you and agree on a date, time, and the service needed.

What should I include in my service request?

Tell us the type of system, what you’ve noticed, and your city or postal code. If you know the equipment brand or model, you can include that too. A short description is enough to start.

Can I discuss pricing before work begins?

Yes. Ask about the visit, any diagnostic charges, and the proposed work when we discuss your request. Costs depend on the equipment and the work required; submitting a request doesn’t approve any work.
